### CHEM-000570: Macroscopic ordered two-dimensional covalent organic frameworks
- **Subfield:** Porous coordination and covalent materials
- **Open:** Grow processable macroscopic or large-area 2D COF crystals with controlled stacking, accessible porosity and preserved order after activation and cycling.
- **Status:** A challenge since the 2000s. Three single-crystal 2D COF isomers were resolved by electron diffraction in 2026; scalable ordered material remains a further target.
- **Type:** engineering
- **Why:** It would connect designed molecular pores to practical adsorption and electronic applications.
